ERIKA Enterprise Manual for Renesas SuperH targets

Introduction

This manual describes the porting details of the ERIKA Enterprise kernel(s) for the Renesas families of microcontrollers which supports 32-bit RISC SH-2A cores (aka SuperH).

Due to customer restrictions, this porting is not yet public. If you want to get more information about the SuperH porting, please contact info@evidence.eu.com.

More details will be available in the near future.
