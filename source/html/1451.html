For research and teaching activities, FLEX boards are used for implementing adaptive embedded control systems.

We investigate, design and implement distributed control systems, ranging from networked control systems to complex automation systems.

We are currently considering to update our labs of the Industrial Communications and Real-Time Systems courses with practical exercises based on FLEX board and its complements.
